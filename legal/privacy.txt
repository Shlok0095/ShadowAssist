SHADOWASSIST — PRIVACY POLICY (SUMMARY)

Last updated: 2026

1. What we collect (locally)
   The application may store on your device: your consent record, settings, encrypted API keys, profile text you enter (such as persona and optional resume or job notes), and similar preferences. Anonymous usage counts may be recorded only if explicitly implemented in a future version; the current focus is local configuration.

2. What we do not collect on our servers
   VeilAssist does not operate a central server that receives your conversations. Audio recordings are not stored for replay. Transcript text and OCR-derived text used during a session are not written to persistent app storage for long-term retention as part of the session pipeline described in the app.

3. Third-party AI (e.g. Groq and others you choose)
   When you send a request, text context (and, for supported providers, an optional screen image) is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you configured using your own key. That provider’s privacy policy applies to that transmission. VeilAssist does not route that data through a VeilAssist-owned cloud in the default configuration.

4. Retention
   Session-oriented buffers are cleared on session end or after prolonged inactivity. Settings and profile data remain until you delete them or uninstall.

5. Your rights
   You may delete local data using in-app controls (for example, “Delete All My Data” where provided). You may export a subset of non-secret profile data where the app offers export.

6. Security
   On Windows, API keys may be protected using the operating system’s secure storage (DPAPI-style encryption where available). Keys are not transmitted except to the providers you configure.

7. Compliance posture
   This policy is written with awareness of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the Information Technology Act, 2000. It is not legal advice.

Contact: use the support channel provided with your distribution, if any.
