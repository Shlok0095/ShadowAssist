FAQ

Is this undetectable?
The app is designed to be discreet on your screen. We do not guarantee that any third party cannot infer assistant use; use only where allowed by your org and the meeting platform.
Do you store my conversations?
Processing goes to the providers you configure. Read the in-app privacy flow and privacy summary.
Why does Windows warn about the file?
New or unsigned apps often trigger SmartScreen. Code signing reduces warnings; always verify SHA256SUMS.txt from the release when you care about integrity.
